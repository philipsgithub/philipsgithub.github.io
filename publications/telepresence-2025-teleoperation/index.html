Towards a Versatile Teleoperation System for Mobile Robots through a Modular Architecture and Scalable Autonomy

S. Bellmann, T. Trapp, E. Hofele, D. Oberacker, M. Grosse Besselmann, N. Spielbauer, P. Keller, L. Puck, T. Schnell, R. Dillmann, A. Roennau

IEEE Conference on Telepresence, 2025

  • DOI (closed access)

At a glance

Domain
Teleoperation and shared autonomy.
Application
Mobile manipulators doing maintenance tasks in factories under remote control.
Challenge
Full autonomy fails in unforeseen situations. Pure teleoperation is repetitive and requires close familiarity with the robot's interface.
Approach
A modular framework with scalable autonomy: manual control with a 3D mouse, adaptable through modes and restrictions, combined with automated subroutines.
Contribution
Shows how scalable autonomy can support the operator instead of competing with them: subroutines take over repetitive steps. Two maintenance-inspired scenarios explore how this eases operation, especially for novice users.
