Low-Compute Trash Segmentation Using Pretrained Vision Transformers Without Fine-Tuning

P. Keller, M. Möck, G. Heppner, R. Dillmann

International Conference on Automation, Robotics and Applications (ICARA), 2025

  • DOI (closed access)

At a glance

Domain
Image segmentation with vision transformers.
Application
Autonomous robots that collect litter outdoors.
Challenge
Litter is so diverse that no dataset covers every item, and trained detectors miss kinds of waste they have not seen.
Approach
Use the attention maps of a frozen, pretrained vision transformer to locate and coarsely segment trash, with an LRP-based distraction branch to look away from common non-trash objects such as small animals.
Contribution
Trash segmentation with no trash-specific training and little compute. On kinds of litter missing from the training data, it holds up better than a trained Mask R-CNN, which stays more precise on the classes it was trained on.
