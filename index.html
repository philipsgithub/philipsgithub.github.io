I work as a research scientist in robotics at the FZI Research Center for Information Technology. There, I have taken several systems from the first idea through the prototype to continuous operation. I like to get hands-on myself, with both software and hardware, and when something fails, I am used to looking for the cause across the whole stack: data, models, high-level control, and hardware drivers.

My deepest expertise is visual perception, especially detecting and tracking objects in camera images. During my PhD with Prof. Rüdiger Dillmann at KIT, completed alongside my job, I worked on recognizing new objects from only a few examples using pretrained models. Beyond perception, I also work on ROS 2 systems engineering, hardware control, and containerized edge deployment on NVIDIA Jetson boards.

Selected research

IROS 2025 & ICIP 2024

Interactive Fine-grained Few-shot Recognition and Detection

Interpretable few-shot recognition with pretrained vision transformers, made more robust for similar classes through optional interactive teaching. Few-shot detection of visually similar tools without fine-tuning, using part prototypes selected interactively by the user and validated on a purpose-built dataset.

Grasping and Retrieving Unknown Hazardous Objects with a Mobile Manipulator

Shared operation for identifying, localizing, and grasping unknown hazardous objects, combined with 3D mapping and evaluated with two mobile robots in a field test.

Performance Evaluation of Real-Time ROS2 Robotic Control in a Time-Synchronized Distributed Network

Evaluation of ROS 2 latency and jitter on single-host and distributed setups, validated with an oscilloscope in an application from object detection to real-time EtherCAT control.
